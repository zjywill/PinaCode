PinaCode privacy policy

Effective November 14, 2026 • iPhone and iPad • Junyi Zhang

Your computer runs the session. Pairing connects the app to the host you choose. Project names, conversations, prompts, attachments, code changes and commands travel between the app and that host. The host keeps project and conversation history. A coding provider configured on that host may receive prompts, code and tool results under its own privacy policy. Direct pairing does not require a PinaCode account. Only pair with hosts you trust.

Device permissions. The camera scans pairing codes. Local Network allows the app to reach a host on your network. Microphone access is used when you record voice input; audio may be processed on your selected host or by the transcription provider you choose. Saving an image can request permission to add it to Photos. The app can use system-protected storage for connection credentials. You can change device permissions in iOS Settings.

Optional services and diagnostics. Depending on host configuration and enabled features, notification services can process device tokens and activity details such as thread names and status. Usage reporting can include a pseudonymous device identifier, product interactions, provider and model, app version, operating system and error or success information. A provider or relay you choose may process connection information. Apple handles TestFlight distribution, feedback and crash reporting under its own policies.

Support and deletion. If you contact support, the developer receives the contact details and information you send to respond. Do not include pairing links, passwords, private source code or API keys. To remove host-side project history or revoke a paired device, use the host or contact its operator. Removing the app does not erase a remote project. Contact the developer for questions about data held for support or to request its deletion: zjywill@gmail.com.
